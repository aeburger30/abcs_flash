## 2024-10-25 - Flashcard Keyboard Shortcuts
**Learning:** Repetitive tasks in flashcard applications, such as sorting items as correct or incorrect, can be tedious when relying on mouse clicks. Keyboard shortcuts offer a significant UX improvement for desktop users.
**Action:** Implement ArrowLeft and ArrowRight keyboard shortcuts for "Wrong" and "Correct" sorting actions, respectively, and update the associated button attributes (`aria-label` and `title`) to make these shortcuts discoverable to all users.
